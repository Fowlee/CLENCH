PRIVACY NOTICE

This explains what CLENCH does with your personal information when you design a mouthguard and book an appointment. Last updated 20 August 2026.

Who is responsible

Clench AS, org. nr 936 281 109, is the data controller. Write to post@clench.no with any question about your information, or to exercise any of the rights below.

What we collect, and why

InformationWhy we need itLegal basis
Name, email, phone To arrange your appointment and reply to your booking Your consent, and performance of a contract
Sport, club, preferred times To fit the guard to your sport and find a time that suits you Your consent
What you tell us about your teeth, and whether you are in orthodontic treatment Braces change how a guard must be made, and an ill-fitting guard is a safety problem Your explicit consent (Article 9(2)(a))
The mouthguard design you make, including any image you upload To produce the guard you asked for Performance of a contract

We do not receive dental scans or impressions through this website. Those are taken at the clinic and handled there. What this site stores is only what you type into the booking form.

Who else sees it

We use three service providers, each acting on our instructions under a data processing agreement:

  • Supabase — stores your booking and your design.
  • Brevo — sends the confirmation email to you and the booking to us. Based in France.
  • Netlify — hosts the website and runs the booking form.

We do not sell your information, and we do not use it for advertising. There is no analytics or tracking on this site, and the only cookie we set is one that keeps CLENCH staff signed in to our own order system. All of this processing takes place within the EU/EEA, so your information is not transferred outside it.

How long we keep it

We keep your booking and design for 24 months after you place it, then delete them. If you ask us to delete them sooner, we will.

Your rights

You can ask us at any time to:

  • send you a copy of everything we hold about you;
  • correct anything that is wrong;
  • delete your information — including withdrawing either of the agreements you gave on the booking form, which you can do without giving a reason and without affecting anything we did before you withdrew it;
  • give you your information in a portable file;
  • restrict or object to what we do with it.

Email post@clench.no and we will respond within one month. If you are not satisfied, you can complain to the Norwegian Data Protection Authority, Datatilsynet.

Your design link

The confirmation email contains a private link to the guard you designed. Anyone holding that link can see the design — though not your name, email or phone number — so treat it as you would any private link. Tell us if you would like it disabled.
