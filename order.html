FAQ

What sports are Clench mouthguards designed for?

Our custom-fit guards are engineered for high-impact and contact sports where protection is non-negotiable:

MMA & Boxing, maximum shock absorption and energy distribution to protect against direct strikes.

Ice Hockey, Lacrosse & Rugby, heavy-duty protection for high-velocity collisions and stick impact.

BJJ & Wrestling, a secure, low-profile fit that allows you to breathe easily and won’t dislodge during grappling.

Football, meets safety standards while fitting comfortably under helmets.

Basketball & General Athletics, ideal for any athlete demanding a dental-grade fit without the bulk of generic guards.

The bottom line: If there is contact, Clench keeps you safe.

Can I order if I already have a dental impression or 3D scan?

Yes. We can skip the impression kit step if you have existing records.

Digital scans, we are integrated with 3Shape. You can send scans directly or email your .stl file to us.

Stone / 3D printed models, if you have physical models, we can provide a shipping label for you to send them to our lab.

This ensures a faster turnaround while maintaining the precision fit Clench is known for.

How long does Clench keep my dental records?

Clench digitizes your impression and stores a record of your teeth in a secure database, so you typically only need to do the impression process once.

Note! If you’ve had new dental work after your last impression, you may need a new one.

If you lose your guard or want a new design, we can produce a new one from your file.

How do I clean and care for my custom mouthguard?

Rinse: Rinse with cold water immediately after every use.

Clean: Use a soft toothbrush with mild soap or a dedicated mouthguard cleaning foam.

Protect: Store it in a ventilated case. Don’t throw it loose into a gym bag.

Avoid heat: Keep away from hot water, dashboards, and direct sunlight to prevent warping.

Pet warning: Keep it away from your dog.

Replace: For maximum protection, replace every 6–12 months depending on usage.

What is the difference between Clench and a “boil-and-bite” guard?

Store-bought boil-and-bite guards are often bulky, can fit loosely, and may obstruct breathing. Clench guards are custom-crafted to the unique shape of your teeth for a lock-tight fit, natural breathing and speech, and significantly better shock absorption.

Can I wear a Clench mouthguard with braces?

Yes. Because teeth can shift during orthodontic treatment, the impression process is slightly different.

Please contact us before ordering so we can guide you to the safest option for both teeth and brackets.
